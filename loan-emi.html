What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed monthly payment made towards repaying a loan, including principal and interest.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n / [(1 + r)^n − 1]

P = Loan principal
r = Monthly interest rate (annual percentage rate divided by 1200)
n = Number of monthly repayments

For zero-interest loans, EMI equals the loan amount divided by the number of months.

This calculator supports any currency, provided all amounts use the same currency.

Results are estimates based on a fixed interest rate, monthly repayments and a reducing loan balance. Fees, taxes and other lender charges are excluded.
